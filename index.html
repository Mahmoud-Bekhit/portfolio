01 · about

Engineer by training.
Data scientist by obsession.

I'm a Computer & Control Engineering graduate. My background in control systems, probability and algorithms shapes how I approach machine learning: model the system, measure everything, and validate before you trust.

That mindset shows up in my work. My plant-disease detector rejects images that aren't leaves before it classifies anything. My employment-impact model was picked by benchmarking 9 regressors and checking cross-validation, not by looking at a single score.

I've trained with GCI World at The University of Tokyo, Egypt's DEPI initiative, Route Academy, Orange Digital Hub and Huawei ICT Academy (HCIA-Big Data). I'm now looking for a team where I can build AI that is scalable and solves real-world problems.

Plant Disease Detection

A two-stage vision system. It first checks that an image really is a plant leaf, then identifies the disease and shows its top-3 predictions with confidence scores.

  • CNN classifier covering 38 healthy and diseased plant categories
  • A separate leaf-detection model rejects irrelevant, non-leaf images
  • Training used augmentation, batch norm, dropout, early stopping and LR scheduling
  • Interactive Gradio app for real-time inference

  • Insights on AI adoption, salaries, job survival and reskilling
